# Pieter Lategan — South African Artist from Pretoria

**Pieter Lategan** is a South African artist based in Pretoria, South Africa.

This repository functions as an official public archive and identity page for Pieter Lategan’s contemporary art practice, including **Silent Monumentalism**, **Gravitas**, structural reliefs, cement surface studies, sculptural objects, visual archives, and contemporary material-based art.

Pieter Lategan has more than 30 years of artistic experience, visual thinking, experimentation, and creative development behind his work.

## Artistic Direction

Pieter Lategan’s work is concerned with:

- structure
- weight
- space
- silence
- material presence
- visual gravity
- architectural abstraction
- authorship
- controlled composition
- emotional and material weight

The work is not decorative or trend-based. It is developed as a serious contemporary art practice rooted in material presence, structure, restraint, and long-term visual investigation.

---

## Silent Monumentalism

**Silent Monumentalism** is a conceptual and visual framework developed through Pieter Lategan’s ongoing investigation into structure, weight, stillness, material, architectural feeling, and controlled visual presence.

Silent Monumentalism is concerned with how form can carry silence, how space can create tension, and how material can hold emotional and physical weight.

---

## Gravitas

**Gravitas** is developing as a discipline within Pieter Lategan’s artistic world.

It focuses on:

- balance
- weight
- proximity
- silence
- structural seriousness
- material presence
- visual gravity
- architectural tension

Gravitas is explored through cement surface studies, structural reliefs, sculptural objects, desk objects, sketchbook studies, and visual archives.

## Short Artist Statement

I am Pieter Lategan, a South African artist from Pretoria.

My work is built around structure, weight, space, material presence, and visual gravity. Through Silent Monumentalism and Gravitas, I investigate how form can hold silence, how material can carry emotional weight, and how space can become part of the structure of an artwork.

My practice includes cement surface studies, structural reliefs, sculptural objects, sketchbook systems, and online archives.

This work is ongoing.
